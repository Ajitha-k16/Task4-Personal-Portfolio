SecureBank Management System

The SecureBank Bank Management System is a software application developed to efficiently manage and automate the core operations of a bank. It provides a secure and user-friendly platform for both bank staff (Admin) and customers to perform their respective activities. In this system, the admin or bank staff can log in to add new customers, create accounts by storing customer details, assigning account numbers, setting opening balances, and generating login credentials. They can also view and edit customer information, perform transactions such as deposits and withdrawals, and monitor all transaction records within the system. On the other hand, customers can log in using their credentials to access their profile, check their account balance, and view their transaction history. The system follows a simple workflow where a new customer first approaches the bank, the staff creates the account and stores all required information, and later the customer can access their account digitally. Overall, the system reduces manual work, improves accuracy, ensures secure data handling, and enhances customer convenience by providing easy access to banking services.
